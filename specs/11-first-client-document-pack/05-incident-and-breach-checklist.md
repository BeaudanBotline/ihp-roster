# Incident and Breach Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist if there is a suspected security incident, privacy incident or data breach.

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Immediate response

- Confirm who identified the incident and when.
- Record the systems, venues and data types potentially affected.
- Contain the issue where possible.
- Preserve logs and evidence.
- Stop any ongoing unauthorised access or disclosure if possible.

## Initial assessment

- Was personal information involved?
- Which venue or venues were affected?
- What categories of information were involved?
- Is payroll-adjacent or sensitive information involved?
- Is there a likely risk of serious harm?

## Internal escalation

- Notify the responsible security contact.
- Notify the responsible privacy/contact person.
- Create an internal incident record with timestamps.

## Investigation

- Identify root cause if possible.
- Identify the affected date range.
- Identify the affected users or workers.
- Identify whether exports, downloads or support access were involved.
- Identify whether third-party services were involved.

## Customer communication

- Decide whether the affected customer should be notified immediately.
- Prepare a plain-language summary of:
  - what happened
  - what data may be affected
  - what containment steps have been taken
  - what the customer should do next

## Legal / notification assessment

- Assess whether any legal notification obligation applies.
- If the Privacy Act applies, assess whether the incident may be an eligible data breach under the OAIC NDB scheme.
- Record the basis for the decision.

## Recovery

- Apply remediation or rollback.
- Rotate credentials or revoke sessions if needed.
- Confirm whether backups are clean and available.
- Document preventive follow-up actions.

## Closure

- Record timeline, impact, decision-making and remediation.
- Update security or privacy controls.
- Update customer-facing documentation if necessary.
- Review whether the same issue could affect other venues.
