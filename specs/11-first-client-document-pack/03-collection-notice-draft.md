## Short worker-facing notice

Your employer uses `[Product name]` to manage rostering, timesheets, leave and related workforce administration.

`[Business name]` provides and supports this software service for your employer. We may collect and handle your personal information, including your name, contact details, account details, roster information, leave information, availability information and timesheet information, to provide and support the service.

We collect this information from you, your employer, or from your use of the service.

We may disclose this information to your employer and its authorised users, and to service providers who help us host, support or secure the service. In some cases, information may also be exported or disclosed to authorised payroll staff, bookkeepers or accountants.

If you do not provide the information requested, some parts of the service may not work properly.

Our privacy policy explains how to contact us, how to request access or correction, and how to make a privacy complaint.

Privacy contact:

- `[contact email]`

## Longer employer-facing collection notice template

The Customer must ensure workers are informed that:

- `[Business name]` provides the software and related support services
- the platform collects and stores worker information for roster, timesheet, leave and related workforce administration purposes
- information may be entered by the employer, managers, payroll staff or the worker
- information may be disclosed to hosting, email, security and support providers used to operate the service
- information may be exported to authorised payroll staff, bookkeepers or accountants where relevant
- workers can ask for access or correction and can make a complaint using the privacy contact details in the privacy policy
