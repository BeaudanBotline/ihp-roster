# First Client Document Pack

## Purpose

This folder contains practical first-pass business and compliance documents for the current operating model:

- local venues
- founder-managed onboarding and support
- standardised multi-venue SaaS
- no public self-serve venue creation

These documents are working drafts, not legal advice.

## Review status guidance

Use these documents as follows:

- operational checklists and internal policy drafts can be used directly after you tailor them to your actual practices
- customer-facing legal documents should be reviewed before signing paying customers

## Documents that should ideally be legally reviewed

- `01-privacy-policy-draft.md`
- `02-customer-terms-draft.md`
- `03-collection-notice-draft.md`

## Documents that are mainly operational drafts

- `04-customer-onboarding-checklist.md`
- `05-incident-and-breach-checklist.md`
- `06-subprocessor-register-template.md`
- `07-retention-schedule-draft.md`

## Minimum recommendation before first paying client

At a practical minimum:

1. Tailor all drafts to your real hosting, support and export practices.
2. Get the privacy policy and customer terms reviewed.
3. Make sure the product actually behaves the way the documents say it behaves.
